GDU Tracker

Growing degree units for corn and soybeans

Weather data provided by Open-Meteo

GDUs use the modified method (50°F base, 86°F cap), counted from the plant date. Projections use the 16-day forecast, then the 10-year average for each day.

This is a reference only. Always confirm crop stage in the field.
